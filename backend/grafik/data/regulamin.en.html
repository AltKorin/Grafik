§ 2. What Grafik is

  1. Grafik is a web application for small businesses (hotels, restaurants, shops, service companies) for planning the team's work schedule, reporting unavailability, submitting and reviewing schedule change requests, and recording clock-ins and clock-outs with NFC: an employee's phone held to an NFC sticker, or a card or key fob held to a reader at a tablet (kiosk).
  2. Grafik shows a summary of planned hours and recorded punches. The summary helps keep working time records; the employer is responsible for keeping and storing those records in accordance with the Polish Labour Code.
  3. An account is created for a company. The person who creates it becomes the company's director and can add employees, departments, login accounts and clock points.
  4. The service is intended for businesses and their employees.

§ 3. Technical requirements

You need an up-to-date web browser with JavaScript enabled, the cookies required for logging in, and an e-mail address. Punching with a phone requires a phone with NFC that opens links from NFC stickers. A kiosk requires a tablet or computer with a browser and an NFC card reader that works like a keyboard, or an Android tablet with Chrome supporting Web NFC.

§ 6. Your obligations

  1. You inform employees that their clock-ins and clock-outs are recorded in Grafik and why (Art. 13 GDPR).
  2. Do not enter special categories of data into Grafik (e.g. medical diagnoses). For unavailability, the type is enough: leave, sick leave or unavailable.
  3. Do not enter unlawful content.

§ 7. Your data in Grafik

  1. The data you enter into Grafik belongs to you. We do not share it with other users or compile statistics from it.
  2. At any time you can download all of the company's data as a JSON file (Company → Download all data) and the hours summary as a CSV file (Working time → Download CSV).
  3. Punches cannot be edited or deleted one by one. A manager's correction is a new entry with a reason and an author, and the original punch stays visible.
